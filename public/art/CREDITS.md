## `shyamala-devi.jpg` — the invocation image

Śyāmalā Devī herself: Rāja Mātaṅgī, seated with the **veena** and her green **parrot** —
the goddess of music and speech invoked before practice.

- **Title:** The goddess Rajamatangi
- **Artist:** Unknown (South Indian, Thanjavur / Company School)
- **Date:** circa 1820–1825
- **Medium:** Painting on paper
- **Licence:** Public domain. Verified Commons tags: `PD-old-100-expired`,
  `PD-Art (PD-old-100-expired)`, `CC-PD-Mark`. The author died more than 100 years ago
  and the work was published before 1 January 1931.
- **Commons file page:** <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rajamatangi.jpg>
- **Direct file URL:** <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/33/Rajamatangi.jpg>
- **Original artwork held by:** Victoria and Albert Museum, London (object
  [O404798](https://collections.vam.ac.uk/item/O404798/painting-the-goddess-rajamatangi/))
- **As shipped:** 377 × 457 px, 122 KB JPEG — the full resolution available on Commons,
  unaltered.

> The V&A serves a larger scan of this same painting, but stamps its own photograph
> "© Victoria and Albert Museum, London". The 1820s painting is out of copyright; that
> particular reproduction was deliberately not used, so every file here rests on an
> explicit Commons public-domain tag.

## `saraswati-ravivarma.jpg` — alternate invocation image

Selectable in Settings for anyone who prefers a larger, more luminous image. Sarasvatī,
also veena in hand.

- **Title:** Goddess Saraswathi
- **Artist:** Raja Ravi Varma (1848–1906)
- **Date:** Oleograph from the Ravi Varma Press, signed "Ravi Varma 1894"; the scanned
  impression is recorded as circa 1930.
- **Medium:** Oleograph / chromolithograph
- **Licence:** Public domain. Verified Commons tags: `PD-old-100-expired`,
  `PD-Art (PD-old-100-expired)`, `CC-PD-Mark`. Also public domain in the United States as
  a work published before 1 January 1931.
- **Commons file page:**
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Raja_Ravi_Varma,_Goddess_Saraswati.jpg>
- **Direct file URL:**
  <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1a/Raja_Ravi_Varma%2C_Goddess_Saraswati.jpg>
- **Original scan source:** The Hemamalini and Ganesh Shivaswamy collection, Bengaluru
- **As shipped:** 1018 × 1400 px, 489 KB JPEG — downscaled from the 7953 × 10936 original.
  No other alteration.
